Why BGR exists

It's not just about who sits together.

Teachers need a fast way to mix students thoughtfully without losing control of classroom dynamics. BGR starts with randomization, then adds the everyday tools that make the groups usable.

≋

Balanced by design

Choose groups of two, three, or four and mix teacher-assigned High, Middle, and Low performance levels—not just names from a hat.

↔

Your classroom rules matter

Keep specific students apart or together while building groups. Teachers remain in control of the constraints.

▦

Real seating charts

Arrange desks into rows, pairs, triangles, or pods. Drag groups around, lock a student in a seat, and populate the room.

⇧

Less time entering rosters

Paste names or import supported grade-report PDFs to build classes and assign initial performance tiers when numeric percentages are available.

◎

Made for active lessons

Mark absent students without reshuffling everyone, pick a random student, and switch to a clean student fullscreen display.

✓

Manual backups, no cloud sync

Keep your classroom workspace on one device, and use manual JSON backup/restore to transfer it when approved. No website account or cloud synchronization.

Manual-only data handling

Class names, student names, tiers, separation rules and seating assignments are stored in this browser's local storage, not automatically synchronized. JSON backups are readable and contain student information; export and transfer them only through school-approved channels. This website cannot protect data from someone with access to the device, browser profile, backups, extensions or malicious scripts. Removing cloud sync does not remove older Firebase data: any previously uploaded cloud records must be reviewed and deleted separately. Clearing website storage deletes local records. The optional PDF import parses locally when the PDF reader is bundled. Follow your school's student-data policies.
